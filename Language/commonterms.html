Common Global Football Terms

General Terms

  • Pitch: The field of play.
  • Goal: When the ball fully crosses the goal line between the posts and under the crossbar.
  • Kick-off: How play begins or restarts after a goal.
  • Half-time: The 15-minute break between the two 45-minute halves of the game.
  • Full-time: The end of the match after 90 minutes (plus stoppage time).

Match Play Terms

  • Dribbling: Controlling and moving the ball past opponents while keeping possession.
  • Pass: Moving the ball from one player to another.
  • Assist: A pass that directly helps set up a goal.
  • Header: Using the head to pass, clear, or score the ball.
  • Tackle: Taking the ball away from an opponent legally.
  • Cross: A pass sent from the wide areas of the pitch into the penalty box.
  • Shot: An attempt to score by kicking or heading the ball toward goal.

Rules and Refereeing Terms

  • Offside: When an attacker is closer to the opponent’s goal than the second-last defender when the ball is played to them.
  • Foul: Breaking the rules through unfair play such as tripping, pushing, or handball.
  • Free Kick: A set-piece awarded after a foul. Can be direct (shot at goal) or indirect (must touch another player).
  • Penalty Kick: A direct shot from the penalty spot after a foul in the penalty area.
  • Throw-in: How play restarts when the ball fully crosses the touchline.
  • Corner Kick: Restart given when the ball crosses the goal line (not in goal) last touched by the defending team.
  • Goal Kick: Restart given when the ball crosses the goal line (not in goal) last touched by the attacking team.

Competition and Player Terms

  • Club: A professional or amateur football team that competes in a league.
  • National Team: A team representing a country in international football.
  • Manager/Coach: The person responsible for tactics, lineups, and team performance.
  • Captain: The player chosen to lead the team on the pitch.
  • Substitute: A player who comes on to replace another during the game.
  • Squad: The full list of players registered to represent a team.
  • Transfer: The process of moving a player from one club to another.
