Iconic Goals in Football History

Diego Maradona — “Goal of the Century” (1986 World Cup)

Against England, Maradona dribbled past five players and scored, cementing his legacy. Often voted the greatest goal ever.

Pelé — 1958 World Cup Final

At just 17, Pelé scored twice against Sweden, including a flick over a defender and volley, launching his legend.

Zinedine Zidane — 2002 Champions League Final

His left-foot volley for Real Madrid against Bayer Leverkusen is one of the most technically perfect goals in UCL history.

Cristiano Ronaldo — Bicycle Kick vs Juventus (2018)

A stunning overhead kick in the Champions League quarterfinal, applauded by Juventus fans themselves.

Lionel Messi — Solo Goal vs Getafe (2007)

Echoed Maradona’s “Goal of the Century” as Messi dribbled from midfield past multiple defenders to score for Barcelona.

Roberto Carlos — Free Kick vs France (1997)

His 35-yard curler with insane swerve defied physics and remains one of the most famous free kicks ever scored.

James Rodríguez — 2014 World Cup Volley

His chest control and left-footed volley for Colombia against Uruguay won the FIFA Puskás Award and global acclaim.

Dennis Bergkamp — vs Argentina (1998 World Cup)

Controlled a long pass with a sublime first touch, cut inside, and finished with precision. A moment of pure artistry.

Gareth Bale — Overhead Kick (2018 UCL Final)

His bicycle kick for Real Madrid against Liverpool in the Champions League final was breathtaking and decisive.

Andrés Iniesta — 2010 World Cup Final

His extra-time strike against the Netherlands won Spain their first World Cup, making it one of the most historic goals ever.
