Basic Operation of Leagues and Tournaments

1. Leagues (Season-Long Competitions)

  • Teams play each other over a full season (home and away).
  • Points system: Win = 3, Draw = 1, Loss = 0.
  • League table ranks teams by points, then goal difference, goals scored, or head-to-head.
  • Champions: Team at the top of the table at the end of the season.
  • Promotion & Relegation: Lowest-ranked teams drop to a lower division, top teams move up.
  • Here's a full overview of how leagues basically work.

2. Knockout Tournaments

  • Teams play elimination matches.
  • Single match: Loser is eliminated.
  • Two-legged ties: Teams play home and away, aggregate score decides winner.
  • Tiebreakers: Extra time (2 × 15 mins), then penalty shootout if still tied.

3. Group Stage Tournaments

  • Teams divided into groups of 3–6.
  • Round-robin format: each team plays all others in the group.
  • Points system: Win = 3, Draw = 1, Loss = 0.
  • Top teams advance to the knockout stage.

4. Hybrid Format (Group + Knockout)

  • Common in major tournaments (World Cup, Euro Cup, AFCON, Copa America).
  • Phase 1: Group stage to filter top teams.
  • Phase 2: Knockout stage until a champion is crowned.

5. Special Rules & Tie-Breakers

  • Away Goals Rule: In two-leg ties, the team with more away goals may advance (less common today).
  • Fair Play Ranking: Used when teams are tied (fewest yellow/red cards).
  • Playoffs: Sometimes used for promotion, relegation, or qualification spots.
