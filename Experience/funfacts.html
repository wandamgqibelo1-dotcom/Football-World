Fun Facts About Football

The World’s Favorite Sport

Football is played in almost every country on Earth, with more than 4 billion fans worldwide. The FIFA World Cup final alone is watched by well over a billion people!

Oldest Football Club

The oldest football club still in existence is Sheffield FC, founded in England in 1857. FIFA officially recognizes it as the world’s first football club.

Biggest Stadium

The Rungrado 1st of May Stadium in North Korea has a capacity of 114,000. But the most famous are the Maracanã in Brazil and Camp Nou in Spain.

The Fastest Goal in World Cup History

Hakan Şükür of Turkey scored after just 11 seconds in the 2002 World Cup against South Korea.

The Highest Scoring Match

In 2002, a match in Madagascar ended 149–0 when SO l’Emyrne deliberately scored own goals to protest refereeing decisions!

Goalkeepers Can Score Too

Brazil’s Rogério Ceni scored over 130 goals as a goalkeeper, mostly from free-kicks and penalties.

Football in Space

A football was taken aboard the International Space Station by NASA astronauts, making football truly universal.
