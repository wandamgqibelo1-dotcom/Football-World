Frequently Asked Questions in Football

A standard football match lasts 90 minutes, split into two halves of 45 minutes each, plus extra time for stoppages. In knockout games, if the match is tied, extra time (2 × 15 minutes) and penalties may be used to decide a winner.

Each team starts with 11 players, including the goalkeeper. Teams can usually make 3–5 substitutions depending on competition rules. If a team falls below 7 players, the match cannot continue.

A player is offside if, at the moment the ball is passed, they are nearer to the opponent’s goal than both the ball and the second-last defender, unless they are in their own half or receiving the ball directly from a goal kick, corner, or throw-in.

Extra minutes are added to make up for stoppages caused by injuries, substitutions, or delays. This is called “added time” or “stoppage time.”

A hat-trick is when a player scores 3 goals in a single match. A “perfect hat-trick” is scoring one goal with the left foot, one with the right foot, and one with the head.
