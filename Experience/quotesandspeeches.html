Famous Quotes and Speeches in Football

Pelé

“Success is no accident. It is hard work, perseverance, learning, studying, sacrifice, and most of all, love of what you are doing.”

Diego Maradona

“When people succeed, it is because of hard work. Luck has nothing to do with success.”

Lionel Messi

“You have to fight to reach your dream. You have to sacrifice and work hard for it.”

Cristiano Ronaldo

“Talent without working hard is nothing.”

Sir Alex Ferguson (Legendary Manchester United Coach)

“Football, bloody hell.”

— said after Manchester United’s dramatic 1999 Champions League victory.

Bill Shankly (Liverpool Manager)

“Some people think football is a matter of life and death. I assure you, it’s much more serious than that.”

José Mourinho (Manager)

“Please don’t call me arrogant, but I’m European champion and I think I’m a special one.”

Arsène Wenger (Arsenal Manager)

“In football, you always have to look forward. What you did yesterday is not important.”
